* [[https://amzn.to/46LhYyg][Unflattening]]

Unflattening is a philosophical contemplation of how our world is increasingly becoming flat and how one can work against this gradual flattening of everything around us. So many cool panels in this one.

* [[https://amzn.to/46LhYyg][Buddha: An Enlightened Life]]

A decent read to understand the life of Buddha. There are certain bits I donʼt agree with in the book like the gimmicks / magic tricks that Buddha performs to impress others. In my understanding he was more of a psychologist than a miracle worker. There are also some supernatural deities which could have been done away with. Overall, I thought it was a really good effort and the part where Buddha uphold his Dhamma and continues his preaching to heal the world despite things going south in the kingdom towards the end was oddly uplifting!

* [[https://amzn.to/34hn9Tz][Logicomix]]
Logicomix to this day remains in my memory as a beautiful exploration of the medium to talk about a serious topic. It features Bertrand Russell, Gödel, Hilbert, and many other logic heroes of 20th century.

* [[https://introducingbooks.com][Introducing Graphic Guides]]
The Introducing Graphic Guide series are a mixed bag of comics towards understanding various subjects. Some are decent/good, others below par. I recommend checking them out somewhere / see if better introductions exist before you purchase them.
